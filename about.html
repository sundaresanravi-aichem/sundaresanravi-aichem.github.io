About Me

I am a Chemistry graduate from SRM Institute of Science and Technology, working at the intersection of computational chemistry, machine learning, and materials science. My experience includes using periodic DFT and molecular quantum-chemical calculations with VASP and Gaussian, alongside Python-based workflows for data-driven chemical and materials research.

I am interested in understanding chemical and materials systems through computational modelling and machine learning, particularly for structure--property prediction and data-driven materials discovery. Questions around sustainable and circular chemistry are among the areas that motivate me to explore how these computational approaches can contribute to broader chemical challenges.

I am still at an early stage of this direction and am using my graduate studies to build deeper foundations in AI, computational chemistry, scientific computing, and data-driven approaches to chemical discovery, while remaining open to where these tools can lead me across chemistry and materials science.

Looking Ahead: I am preparing for Master's study in Germany for the 2027 intake, with the goal of strengthening my foundations in computational chemistry, AI, and scientific data science while exploring research directions across chemistry, materials science, and sustainable chemical systems.
